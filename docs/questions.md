# InsureClear user questions

| # | Question | Can InsureClear answer it? | Topic / reason |
|---|----------|----------------------------|----------------|
| 1 | I got my LIC policy by post 10 days back and I don't like it. Can I return it and get my money back? | Yes | Free-Look Period |
| 2 | Insurance company rejected my claim because I informed them 3 days late. Is that allowed? | Yes | Rejection of Claims |
| 3 | What is portability in health insurance and can I keep my waiting period benefit? | Yes | Portability |
| 4 | I bought a policy online on 20 October. Till which date can I cancel it under free-look, and how much refund will I get on a 18,500 premium? | Yes, with the free-look calculator | Free-Look Period + calculator |
| 5 | My hospital claim was rejected and the company is not replying. What are the steps to complain, and when can I go to the Insurance Ombudsman? | Yes | Grievance, Bima Bharosa, Ombudsman |
| 6 | Which health insurance company has the best claim settlement ratio in Maharashtra this year? | **No** | Not in the data. The IRDAI consumer pages do not rank companies or give state-wise settlement ratios |
| 7 | My health insurance renewal is two weeks late. Is my policy cancelled? | Yes | Renewability and grace period |
| 8 | Is my mother's hospital bill covered if I already have a group policy through my school? | Partly | Group Insurance explains the rules, but covered items depend on my policy document |
| 9 | I sold my policy details to an agent over the phone and bought a plan. Can I still cancel it? | Yes | Distance Marketing (30-day free look) |
| 10 | Which hospital in Ahmednagar will accept my cashless claim? | **No** | Hospital network lists belong to each insurer and are not on the IRDAI pages |
